AI Terms Flashcards

12 cards, one 25:00 session, 2:00 max per card.

How the session works

  • Your session clock starts at 25:00 and ends the instant it reaches 0:00. A card on screen at that moment is discarded.
  • Each card shows either the term or the definition, chosen at random. Every term is shown both ways over the session.
  • Recall the hidden side, press Reveal, then grade yourself: Knew it or Missed it.
  • Each card has 2:00. If it runs out, the answer is revealed and the card counts as Missed.
  • Pass 1 is all 12 cards in random order. Each later pass reshuffles all 12, and any card you Missed in the previous pass appears twice. The last card of one pass never opens the next.
  • Keys: Space reveal / next, 1 or K knew it, 2 or M missed it.

Deck and sources

Definitions were cross-checked against up to 4 web sources each. No CS 215 course material was available to me, so course-first priority was not applied. Review the definitions here before you start: they will be hidden during the session. Terms marked with a dagger have fewer than 4 supporting sources.
